## 3) Reddit

⚠️ **Read each subreddit's rules first.** Most ban or heavily restrict self-promotion. Don't post the same
text in all three (Reddit flags cross-posting spam). Engage as a person, comment elsewhere first to build
account history, and disclose that you built it. Best posting time for India: 7–10 PM IST, weekdays.

### r/developersIndia (most receptive — tech/build audience)
Check rules; many subs require a "Showoff Saturday" or self-promo thread. Frame as a build story.

**Title:** I built an AI resume builder that scores your ATS match before you apply (solo, Next.js + Supabase)

**Body:**
> A few months ago I watched a friend apply to 40+ roles with one generic resume and hear nothing. The
> issue wasn't him — it was that most resumes get filtered by ATS keyword matching before a human reads
> them, and tailoring a resume per role manually is painful.
>
> So I built **CV Prime** (https://cv-prime.in). You paste a job description, it tailors your bullets to
> the role, scores your ATS match and shows the exact keyword gaps, then exports a clean PDF.
>
> Stack: Next.js 14 (App Router), Supabase, Tailwind, OpenRouter (BYOK so users control AI spend),
> Razorpay for payments. Happy to go into the technical details — ATS scoring, the BYOK encryption setup,
> PDF export via Browserless, whatever's useful.
>
> It's free to start (no card). Would genuinely love feedback from this sub on the tailoring quality and
> what you'd want from a tool like this. Roast it.

### r/cscareerquestionsIndia (focus on the job-search pain, not the product)
This sub is strict about self-promo. Only post if rules allow; lead with help, mention the tool lightly.

**Title:** Most resumes get filtered by ATS before a human sees them — here's what actually helped

**Body:**
> After a lot of "applied to X jobs, zero callbacks" posts here, I want to share what I learned about ATS
> filtering while building a tool around it:
>
> - One generic resume for every role is the #1 mistake. ATS ranks you on keyword + skill match to *that*
>   job description.
> - Mirror the exact phrasing from the JD (e.g. "REST APIs" vs "RESTful services") — ATS often doesn't
>   treat them as the same.
> - Quantify bullets (impact + number). Avoid tables, columns, and graphics that break ATS parsing.
> - Tailor per application. Yes it's tedious — that's the real bottleneck.
>
> I built a free tool to automate the tailoring + scoring part (CV Prime — https://cv-prime.in), but even
> if you don't use it, the principles above are what moved the needle for people I helped. Happy to answer
> ATS / resume questions in the comments.
