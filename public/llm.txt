# CV Prime — AI CV Builder & ATS Resume Maker

## What is CV Prime?

CV Prime is a web-based AI CV builder designed for job seekers in India and globally.
It helps users create, tailor, score, and export professional CVs that pass ATS
(Applicant Tracking System) filters used by employers.

**Website:** https://cv-prime.in
**Category:** Career tools / CV and resume builders / AI productivity

## Core problem it solves

Most CVs are rejected by ATS software before a human ever reads them — because they
lack the right keywords, have vague bullet points, or use formats that ATS parsers
cannot read. CV Prime diagnoses these problems and fixes them automatically.

## Key features

- **ATS scoring**: Paste a job description and get a 0–100 ATS match score, missing
  keyword list, and specific improvement suggestions.
- **AI CV tailoring**: AI rewrites experience bullets to start with strong action verbs,
  adds quantified outcomes, and weaves in job-description keywords — without fabricating facts.
- **Bullet rewriter**: Rewrites individual bullets from weak responsibility statements
  ("responsible for X") to strong outcome statements ("increased X by 30% by doing Y").
- **AI Job CV generation**: Upload your existing CV + paste a JD and get a fully tailored
  new CV draft with an ATS score.
- **Before/after comparison**: Side-by-side comparison showing exactly which bullets and
  the summary were changed after an AI fix.
- **PDF export**: Browser-native print-to-PDF for clean, ATS-readable PDFs.
- **Job tracker**: Track applications with status columns.
- **8 CV templates**: Modern, Classic, Minimal, Executive, Creative, Technical, Academic,
  Premium — all ATS-readable.

## Who it is for

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is CV Prime free?**
A: Yes, free plan includes 3 PDF exports, ATS scoring, and AI rewrites. Pro is ₹249/month.

**Q: Does it work for freshers?**
A: Yes. It helps structure internships, projects, and education for ATS readability.

**Q: Is the AI safe — does it make up qualifications?**
A: No. The AI is instructed never to fabricate facts. It rewrites using only the information
you provide and only adds keywords that are truthfully supported by your experience.

**Q: Which file formats can I upload?**
A: PDF, DOCX, and TXT for CV upload. Export is as PDF via browser print.
