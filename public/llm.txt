# CV Prime — AI CV Builder, ATS Resume Maker & Career Intelligence Platform

## What is CV Prime?

CV Prime is a web-based AI-powered resume builder, CV maker, ATS optimizer, cover letter generator, and career intelligence platform designed primarily for Indian job seekers and usable globally. It helps users create, tailor, score, and export professional CVs that pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) used by employers.

## Core Problem CV Prime Solves

75% of CVs are rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) before a human recruiter ever reads them. The primary causes:
1. Missing or mismatched keywords (55% of ATS rejections)
2. Non-parseable formatting (tables, columns, text boxes, graphics)
3. Vague bullets that describe duties instead of outcomes
4. Generic CVs sent to every role without tailoring

CV Prime diagnoses all of these problems automatically and fixes them with AI.

## Key Features

### 1. ATS Scoring (0–100 Score)
Paste a job description and instantly receive:
- ATS compatibility score (0–100)
- List of missing keywords from the JD
- Section-by-section improvement suggestions
- Keyword match percentage

### 2. AI CV Tailoring
The AI rewrites experience bullets to:
- Start with strong action verbs (Increased, Reduced, Led, Built, Designed)
- Add quantified outcomes (%, ₹, numbers, timeframes)
- Weave in job-description-specific keywords
- Never fabricate facts — only rewrites what the user has provided

### 3. Bullet Rewriter
Converts weak responsibility statements to strong impact statements:
- Before: "Responsible for managing the analytics team"
- After: "Led 6-person analytics team that reduced reporting cycle from 5 days to 1 day, enabling same-day business decisions"

### 4. AI Job CV Generation
Upload existing CV + paste job description → receive a fully tailored new CV draft with ATS score. Powered by OpenRouter (users connect their own API key for maximum privacy).

### 5. Before/After Comparison
Side-by-side view showing exactly which bullets and sections changed after AI optimization.

### 6. CV Templates (8 Total)
All templates are ATS-readable (no tables, columns, or graphics that break parsers):
- Modern — clean two-column layout for tech and business
- Classic — traditional single-column for conservative industries
- Minimal — whitespace-focused for senior executives
- Executive — prestige layout for C-suite and director roles
- Creative — structured layout with colour accent for design/marketing
- Technical — skills-first layout for engineering and data roles
- Academic — publication and research-focused for academia
- Premium — polished gradient layout for premium positioning

### 7. PDF Export
Browser-native print-to-PDF producing clean, ATS-readable PDFs without rasterization or image-based rendering.

### 8. Cover Letter Generator
AI-powered cover letter generation tailored to the specific job description and company.

### 9. Job Tracker
Kanban-style application tracker to manage the entire job search in one place.

### 10. CV Upload & Parse
Upload existing CV in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format. CV Prime extracts and structures the content automatically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is CV Prime free?**
A: Yes. The free plan includes 3 PDF exports, ATS scoring and analysis, AI bullet rewrites, cover letter generation, all 8 CV templates, and the job tracker. No credit card required. The Pro plan (₹249/month) unlocks unlimited exports and removes the watermark.

**Q: Does CV Prime work for freshers with no work experience?**
A: Yes. CV Prime helps structure internship experience, academic projects, certifications, extracurriculars, and skills sections for maximum ATS readability. It is specifically effective for campus recruitment and fresher roles.

**Q: Is the AI safe — does it fabricate qualifications?**
A: No. CV Prime's AI is explicitly instructed never to fabricate, invent, or assume facts. It only rewrites using information the user provides. Keywords are added only if they can be truthfully inferred from existing experience. The goal is authentic optimization, not deception.

**Q: Which file formats can I upload?**
A: PDF, DOCX (Microsoft Word), and plain text (.txt) files. The parser extracts your existing CV content automatically.

**Q: Does CV Prime work for international job applications?**
A: Yes. While CV Prime is optimized for India, the ATS scoring, AI tailoring, and templates work for applications to companies in the UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, the US, and Singapore. The content library includes India-specific guidance but the core tools are globally applicable.

**Q: What is ATS and why does it matter?**
A: ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by companies to screen job applications automatically before a human recruiter reviews them. 98% of Fortune 500 companies use ATS. Over 90% of large Indian employers use ATS. CVs that fail ATS screening are never seen by a human — regardless of how qualified the candidate is.

**Q: How does CV Prime differ from a regular CV builder?**
A: Traditional CV builders (like Canva or Word templates) only help with formatting. CV Prime goes further: it analyzes your CV against a specific job description, scores the ATS match, identifies missing keywords, and uses AI to rewrite bullets to increase your match score. It's an ATS optimizer and AI coach, not just a formatting tool.

**Q: What makes a CV ATS-friendly?**
A: ATS-friendly CVs have: (1) standard section headings (Work Experience, Education, Skills — not creative alternatives), (2) simple single-column layouts without tables, text boxes, or graphics, (3) keywords that match the job description, (4) outcome-driven bullets with quantified achievements, (5) standard fonts and no images.

**Q: Can I use CV Prime for a cover letter?**
A: Yes. CV Prime includes an AI cover letter generator that creates a tailored cover letter based on your CV content and the specific job description you're targeting.

**Q: Is my data safe on CV Prime?**
A: CV Prime uses Supabase for secure data storage. For AI processing, users connect their own OpenRouter API key — meaning your CV content goes to the AI provider you choose, not through CV Prime's servers. API keys are encrypted with AES-256 before storage.
