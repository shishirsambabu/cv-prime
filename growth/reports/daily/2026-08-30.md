# Growth report — 2026-08-30

First run. Establishes the system, the baseline, and an honest account of
what can and cannot be seen.

## Headline

The highest-impact action available today was not a campaign or a page. It
was that **the top of the funnel could not be measured at all**, so no
prioritisation downstream of it would have been trustworthy. That is now
fixed, and the two verified technical SEO defects found along the way are
fixed with it.

## Diagnostic

### 01 — Funnel instrumentation
**9 of 13 funnel steps were instrumented; now 13 of 13.**

Four steps were blind:

- All 19 free tools emitted no events whatsoever. They sit at the top of the
  acquisition funnel, reachable from ~145 indexable pages, and nothing about
  their usage or conversion was recorded.
- "Resume completed" — a north-star funnel stage — had no event, so the drop
  between creating a resume and exporting one could not be located.

A fifth problem was subtler and mattered more. `user_signed_up` recorded
`source: 'email' | 'google'` — the *login button*, not the acquisition
source. In a dashboard that reads like attribution. It meant the central
question for a site of this shape, *which of our pages actually earns
users*, was unanswerable, while appearing to be answered.

**Fixed.** First-touch attribution (landing page, referrer, UTMs) is now
captured on a visitor's first page view and attached to the seven outcome
events, so page → signup → resume → export → revenue can be traced end to
end. `tool_run_started`, `tool_run_completed`, `tool_run_gated` and
`cv_completed` are new. All 14 gated AI tools were covered through the one
hook they share.

### 02 — Crawlable surface
145 indexable static pages; sitemap coverage now complete. Two defects
found and fixed:

- `/create-with-ai` is a bare redirect to `/signup` but was submitted at
  priority 0.8. Submitted redirects earn a "Page with redirect" in Search
  Console and spend crawl budget for nothing.
- `/cv-prime-vs-linkedin-resume` — a commercial-intent comparison page —
  was the only one of 20 such pages missing from the sitemap.

### 03 — Metadata
154 pages audited. Two pages export no metadata: `/signup` (falls back to
the root template; low priority, it is not an SEO target) and
`/create-with-ai` (a redirect, correctly has none).

130 titles exceed 60 characters and 134 descriptions exceed 160. **No bulk
rewrite was performed, deliberately.** That the pattern covers ~85% of pages
means it is a house style, not 130 mistakes, and Google truncates on pixel
width regardless. Rewriting site-wide metadata with no CTR baseline is
precisely the unmeasured change this system exists to prevent. Logged as
GROW-004, blocked on Search Console, to be executed against the ~15 pages
with the largest impressions × CTR gap once that data exists.

An earlier version of this audit reported 20 pages with critically thin
descriptions. All 20 were a regex bug in the audit itself — an escaped
apostrophe truncated the match. Caught and fixed before it reached the
backlog; recorded in `learnings.md`.

### 04–10 — Search intent, community, lifecycle, competitors, experiments
Not run. Each depends on data sources that are not connected, and running
them on assumption would produce confident-sounding fiction. The routines
are defined in `growth/agents/README.md` and activate when GROW-005 lands.

One experiment is written and deliberately **not started**: EXP-001 proposes
reducing the AI tool gate friction, and states its own start condition —
two weeks of gate data showing `AUTH` as the dominant gate. Building it
before that risks solving the wrong gate.
